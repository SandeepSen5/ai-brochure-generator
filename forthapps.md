# Forth Apps Brochure

## About Us

Forth Apps is a dynamic software company founded in 2021 by a team of passionate developers and designers dedicated to building innovative ERP and HRMS solutions. Our mission is to empower businesses and individuals through cutting-edge software applications, driven by a relentless pursuit of quality and innovation.

## Our Products

We offer a suite of specialized systems designed to address the unique needs of various industries and businesses:

*   **ForthERP**: An all-in-one ERP platform covering sales, inventory, payroll, and business insights for enterprises, SMEs, and growing teams.
*   **PharmaERP**: A purpose-built ERP for pharmacies and clinics with prescription tracking and Tatmeen integration.
*   **ForthBooks**: Automates invoicing, multi-currency accounting, and payroll from a single dashboard.
*   **PharmaBooks**: Pharmacy-focused accounting with inventory tracking and built-in financial reporting.
*   **ForthHRMS**: Automates attendance, payroll, and employee management with AI-powered HR tools.
*   **ForthCRM**: Manages leads, follow-ups, tasks, deals, and sales activities from one smart CRM platform.
