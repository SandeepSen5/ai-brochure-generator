## Our Products

We offer a range of products designed to cater to the diverse needs of various industries. Our flagship products include:

* **ForthERP**: An all-in-one ERP platform that manages sales, inventory, payroll, and business insights across all branches.
* **PharmaERP**: A purpose-built ERP for pharmacies and clinics, designed to streamline prescription tracking, doctor & clinic management, and financial reporting.
* **ForthBooks**: An accounting platform that automates invoicing, multi-currency accounting, and payroll from a single dashboard.
* **PharmaBooks**: A pharmacy-focused accounting solution with inventory tracking and built-in financial reporting.
* **ForthHRMS**: An AI-powered HR system that automates attendance, payroll, and employee management.
* **ForthCRM**: A smart CRM platform that manages leads, follow-ups, tasks, deals, and sales activities.
